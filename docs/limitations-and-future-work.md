# Limitations and Future Work

The paper identifies several barriers that must be addressed before an AI-powered cardiovascular digital twin could be used routinely in the NHS.

## Key limitations and challenges

- heterogeneous clinical systems and interoperability requirements
- variation in digital maturity across NHS Trusts
- privacy and security requirements for sensitive health information
- limited clinical evidence for many digital-twin applications
- risks of bias when training data are not representative
- need for explainable outputs and clinician trust
- workflow integration under real clinical workload
- scalability and monitoring over the model lifecycle

## Future directions

The paper proposes further validation of the framework and expansion of cardiovascular applications. It also identifies potential future application to oncology, respiratory conditions and multimorbidity management once appropriate evidence and validation are available.

The longer-term vision is a learning health system in which patient information can support increasingly personalised and data-driven care while maintaining governance, interoperability and human oversight.
