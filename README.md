## 📖 Abstract

This work presents an AI-powered digital twin framework for personalised healthcare delivery within the UK National Health Service (NHS). The proposed system integrates multimodal patient data — including electronic health records (EHRs), laboratory results, medical imaging, and wearable device streams — into a dynamic, real-time digital twin model. By leveraging artificial intelligence, the framework enables predictive analytics, personalised treatment pathways, and proactive health monitoring tailored to individual patient profiles. The architecture addresses key challenges in NHS data interoperability and scalability, offering a scalable approach to precision medicine and improved clinical decision-making.
